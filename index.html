About us

A technology company born on the shop floor.

Founded in April 2023 in Vitória, Brazil, by military engineers in metallurgy and occupational safety, together with a postdoctoral researcher in genetics, all with extensive international experience in industry and academia.

We are part of NEXUS Growth in São José dos Campos, Brazil, the country's most vibrant technology ecosystem.

Mission

To develop technologies that anticipate risk.

Vision

Operations where people and processes adjust in synergy, moment by moment: maximum safety for those who work, maximum efficiency for those who produce.
