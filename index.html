Risk Nowcasting

See where risk is building up across your site — before the storm breaks.

MapOmega builds Risk Nowcasting solutions for heavy operations. Every report, sensor and system is placed where it happened, weighed by severity and context, and governed until it's fixed.

Risk = severity × context × accumulation over time
The problem

More data than ever. Still no forecast.

Sites already capture inspections, incidents, near misses, camera detections and maintenance orders. What's missing is the reading of all of it — where, how severe, and what to fix first.

01

Reports pile up unread

Every report gets filed. Patterns surface weeks later, in a monthly review — if at all.

02

Alarms without context

Detection without weighting floods field teams with alerts. The one that matters gets lost in the noise.

03

Silos never meet

Safety, quality and maintenance each live in their own system. When they converge on the same spot, nobody sees it.

The difference

Same event. Different risk.

Detection tells you something happened. Nowcasting tells you whether it matters — here. Each zone of your site carries the context your safety team defines: hazards, activity, people exposed.

Where reports should exist and don't, that's a signal too.

A hundred reports of paper on the floor weigh less than one report of a fire hazard. Risk is severity in context, accumulating over time — and it clears when the fix is made.
