FAQ

Before you ask.

Is MapOmega Core a certified measuring instrument?

No — and that isn't its role. Legal proof of compliance still requires the official survey, with a calibrated instrument. MapOmega Core covers the gap between one survey and the next, showing who is most exposed day to day.

Does it replace certified measurement?

No. It shortens the blind interval between reports, with continuous readings aligned to the standard's thresholds — but the legal proof of compliance remains the official survey.

Where is the data stored?

In the cloud, by default. On-premises hosting can be evaluated after a technical assessment with your IT team.

Does it integrate with our timekeeping system?

Yes, via REST API — exposure is automatically linked to shift and operator.

Does it only work with these two sensors?

Today we have two formats in the field — a chest sensor with an app, and a standalone belt clip. The platform was designed to take in other sources as your operation requires.
